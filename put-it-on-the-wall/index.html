The manifesto / 001

Organisations don’t have a shortage of intelligence.

They have a shortage of shared understanding.

When the whole picture cannot be seen, decisions get made from fragments. Complexity gets hidden behind status updates. Work multiplies. Meetings repeat.

The answer is not another presentation about the problem.

Make the problem visible.
Make it collective.
Make it possible to change.

Why transformation stalls / 002

You cannot solve what the room cannot see.

Most transformation fails long before delivery. It fails when the organisation creates an incomplete picture of itself—and calls it alignment.

01

The truth is fragmented

Strategy lives in one deck. Delivery in another. Risk in a spreadsheet. Reality in people’s heads.

02

The room is too small

A handful of people simplify the problem, then present the answer to everyone expected to deliver it.

03

The format is wrong

Slides force complexity into a sequence. Organisations are systems. Systems do not happen one slide at a time.
