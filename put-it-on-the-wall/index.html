Questions worth asking

No theatre. No mystery.

Does every programme end with an AI recommendation?

No. A credible No-AI decision is a valuable outcome. The purpose is to choose the intervention that creates value, not to justify a technology already selected.

Is this consultancy or a toolkit?

Neither in the usual sense. It is a fully designed team journey: Rose frames the moments that require expert judgement, while an internal facilitator operates the runbook inside the organisation.

Can the WALL method be used beyond AI?

Yes. WALL is a problem-to-action method. AI is the first market entry because the pressure is urgent; future applications will only be launched when the first product has repeatable proof.

What happens in a first conversation?

We test whether the problem is significant, cross-functional and decision-ready; whether there is a sponsor carrying the risk; and whether a founding team programme is the right next move.
