Privacy and analytics

Your information should stay useful, proportionate and protected.

This website does not use a customer account or contact form. When you choose an email link, your email application handles the message you send. Optional analytics are switched off unless you actively accept them.

Information you send

If you contact Put It On The Wall by email, the information you provide will be used to respond to your enquiry, assess whether the programme is a fit and maintain an appropriate record of the conversation.

Analytics choices

With your permission, the site loads Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits and interactions such as opening an FAQ, viewing the WALL method or selecting an email call to action. Analytics does not load before you accept. Your choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember it.

TechnologyPurposeWhen used
Local storageRemembers whether you accepted or declined analytics.When you make a choice.
Google Analytics 4Provides aggregated information about site use and conversion actions.Only after you accept analytics.

You can reopen the choice panel using “Analytics choices” in the website footer. Declining analytics does not affect access to the site.

Legal basis and retention

Enquiry information is processed where necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, and for legitimate business interests in managing genuine commercial relationships. Optional analytics are used on the basis of your consent. Information is retained only for as long as it remains relevant or legally necessary.

Sharing and international processing

Your information is not sold. It may be processed by necessary business service providers, including email, hosting and analytics providers, subject to appropriate contractual and data-protection safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection in relation to your personal information, or withdraw consent for optional analytics.

Contact
