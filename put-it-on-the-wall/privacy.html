Privacy

Your information should stay useful, proportionate and protected.

This website does not currently use a contact form, customer account or advertising cookies. When you choose an email link, your email application handles the message you send.

Information you send

If you contact Put It On The Wall by email, the information you provide will be used to respond to your enquiry, assess whether the programme is a fit and maintain an appropriate record of the conversation.

Legal basis and retention

Enquiry information is processed where necessary to take steps at your request before entering a contract, and for legitimate business interests in managing genuine commercial relationships. Information is retained only for as long as it remains relevant or legally necessary.

Sharing

Your information is not sold. It may be processed by necessary business service providers, such as email and hosting providers, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection in relation to your personal information.
