# Put It On The Wall™ — MVP brand system

## Brand role

**Master brand and category:** Put It On The Wall  
**Method:** WALL  
**First product:** AI Opportunity Wall  
**Authority asset:** The Data Narrator  

The brand exists to make complex business reality visible before organisations commit to AI, technology or transformation spend.

## Positioning

**Category line:** Problem-first strategy for the age of AI.  
**Primary proposition:** Find the business problems worth solving before choosing the AI.  
**Hero line:** Before you choose the AI, put the problem on the wall.  
**Promise:** See the problem. Make the decision. Move the business.  

## Audience

The primary buyer is the person carrying the decision risk: COO, CDAO, Transformation Director, Chief Digital Officer, Strategy Director or business-unit MD. The first market wedge is aerospace, advanced engineering, manufacturing and other regulated or operationally complex organisations.

## Personality

- Candid, not cynical
- Precise, not academic
- Provocative, not performative
- Practical, not simplistic
- Authoritative, not consultant-dependent

## Voice rules

1. Lead with the business problem, never the technology.
2. Use short, declarative sentences and visible choices.
3. Name the enemy clearly: AI theatre, vendor-led agendas and pilots without value.
4. Preserve nuance in the method, but make the decision language simple: pursue, fix, defer or stop.
5. Never imply that every problem needs AI.

## Visual direction

**Creative territory:** editorial-industrial. The identity combines a serious leadership publication with the physical evidence of a working wall: grid lines, tape, marker strokes, numbered cards and quality gates.

Avoid conventional AI imagery, glowing gradients, robots, circuit patterns, stock photography of workshops and generic sticky-note collages.

### Colour

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Ink | `#151510` |
| Ground | Wall white | `#F3EFE4` |
| Secondary ground | Plaster | `#E4DDCE` |
| Signal | Decision yellow | `#E8FF3F` |
| Evidence/action | Marker red | `#EF3B2D` |
| Light contrast | Clean white | `#FFFDF7` |

Signal yellow should be used for action, invitation and decision moments. Marker red should be used for challenge, evidence and emphasis. Neither should become a decorative wash across the whole brand.

### Typography

- **Headlines / wordmark:** Archivo, heavy weights, tightly tracked, usually uppercase.
- **Labels / navigation / evidence tags:** IBM Plex Mono.
- **Body:** Archivo regular, with generous spacing and short line lengths.

### Wordmark

The MVP wordmark is stacked and direct:

```
PUT IT
ON THE
WALL
```

`WALL` may sit in an ink block with decision-yellow type. Do not add a standalone abstract AI symbol. The name is the mnemonic.

## Founder relationship

Rose is the author and authority behind the system, but Put It On The Wall must not look like a renamed consulting service. Use “Created by Rose Attridge” and selective proof. The method, product and customer outcome lead; Rose validates them.

## MVP website job

The first website has one job: recruit and qualify founding-team conversations for AI Opportunity Wall. It should not advertise future product rooms, a practitioner pathway, SaaS, benchmarking or a broad transformation ecosystem before proof exists.

## Initial conversion

**Primary CTA:** Apply as a founding team.  
**Secondary CTA:** See the WALL method.  
**Qualification prompt:** What is the organisation trying to decide, why does it matter now and who carries the decision risk?
