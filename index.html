02 · How I help

From difficult material to usable clarity

Clear thinking, structured evidence and a decision people can carry.

The shape of the engagement depends on the problem. The constant is a rigorous search for what is really happening, followed by a form that makes action possible.

03 · Evidence and experience

Experience beneath the language

Built in organisations where the answer had consequences.

My career spans business improvement, customer experience, privacy, data governance, strategy and complex transformation.

I have worked across engineering, construction, insurance, retail and energy—inside environments where decisions had operational, commercial and regulatory consequences. The recurring task was to understand the system, expose the important pattern and help capable people move together.
